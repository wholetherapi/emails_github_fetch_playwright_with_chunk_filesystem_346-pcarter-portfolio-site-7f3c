We study cascade-style error bounds for sketch-and-project methods on ill-conditioned linear systems. The analysis separates the sketching error from the projection contraction and yields iteration-complexity guarantees that hold uniformly across row and column sketches. Numerical experiments confirm the predicted cascade constants on a suite of sparse benchmark problems.
